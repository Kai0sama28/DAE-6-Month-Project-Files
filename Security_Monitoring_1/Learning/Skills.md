1. List of Skills for AI 

**Analytical & Critical Thinking**: Quickly evaluating command lines, behavioral context, and patterns to understand why an event matters rather than just reading a rule match.

**True vs. False Positive Discrimination**: Determining if an alert stems from authorized activity (like a scheduled scan) or malicious behavior, and knowing how to tune rules to cut noise.

**Log & Telemetry Interpretation**: Pulling and correlating data from SIEM, EDR, network logs, and identity providers to trace the blast radius.

**Threat Intelligence & IOC Enrichment**: Mapping observables like IP addresses, file hashes, domain names, and user identities against threat feeds.

**Framework Familiarity**: Structuring classifications using standards like NIST SP 800-61 or SANS PICERL and mapping tactics to MITRE ATT&CK.

**The 5 Core Pillars of Cybersecurity**

*Change*: Constantly updating systems, managing software patches, and monitoring threats because hackers and digital tools evolve rapidly. 


*Compliance*: Adhering to relevant local and international data protection laws and standards (such as GDPR, HIPAA, or PCI DSS) to avoid legal penalties and build customer trust. 


*Cost*: Balancing the financial investment in security tools and managed services against the much higher potential cost of a data breach or system shutdown. 


*Continuity*: Creating a business continuity and disaster recovery plan, including regular data backups and incident response steps, to keep operations running after an attack. 


*Coverage*: Securing every layer of the digital infrastructure, including endpoints (laptops and phones), cloud platforms, networks, email systems, and identity access points.
