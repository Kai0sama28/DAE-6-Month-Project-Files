6. Attack Narrative

At 17:46 the host recorded three lightdm graphical login sessions (user 'lightdm') that closed at 17:53. Beginning at 17:55:46, a local process initiated rapid SSH password-guessing attempts against the 'labtester' account, generating 33 sshd authentication failures (rule 5760 firedtimes) and 8 PAM failures (rule 5503 firedtimes) within 12 seconds. Wazuh rule 100002 triggered at 17:55:48 (firedtimes=5) and continued incrementing. After a ~16-minute pause, a second burst of 16 sshd failures and 4 PAM failures occurred at 18:12:47–18:13:01, again triggering rule 100002. All attempts originated from 192.168.64.3 (the Kali agent itself). No successful login, privilege change, or file modification was recorded.
