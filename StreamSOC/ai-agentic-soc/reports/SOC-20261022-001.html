1. Executive Summary

A sustained SSH brute-force attack from internal host 192.168.64.1 targeted the account 'labtester' on the Kali agent (192.168.64.3) over a 27-minute window (16:25:38–16:53:06 UTC). The attack occurred in three distinct bursts of ~34 seconds each, with attempts roughly every 2 seconds. Wazuh rule 100002 (level 12, verified SSH brute force) fired six times, and no successful authentication was observed. The source IP is internal (RFC1918), raising the possibility of an insider threat or compromised internal host, though the target hostname 'Kali' and account 'labtester' suggest a potential authorized test environment.

The first burst ended at 16:26:12 after rule 100002 triggered twice (firedtimes 7–8). A second burst started at 16:34:55, again at 2-second intervals, with rule 100002 firing at 16:35:11 and 16:35:29 (firedtimes 9–10). A third burst began at 16:52:32; rule 100002 fired at 16:52:48 (firedtimes 11) and the trigger alert at 16:53:06 (firedtimes 12). Throughout the 72 correlated events, only authentication-failure rules (5503, 5760, 5551, 100002) appeared; no success, privilege-change, file-integrity, or session events were recorded.
