6. Attack Narrative

The Kali host (192.168.64.3) issued WPAD lookups via LLMNR and NBT-NS. The attacker at 192.168.64.50 responded with poisoned responses for both protocols within milliseconds, while simultaneously spoofing ARP replies claiming to be the gateway (192.168.64.1) from MAC 02:de:ad:be:ef:50. This traffic redirection led to an SMB NTLMSSP authentication attempt from the victim to the attacker IP, consistent with credential capture or relay. The Wazuh agent on the victim stopped at 16:16:59 and restarted at 16:17:00, possibly due to network disruption or deliberate impairment.
