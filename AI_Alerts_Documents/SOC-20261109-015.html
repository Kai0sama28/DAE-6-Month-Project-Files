6. Attack Narrative

At 16:16:43 UTC the Kali host (192.168.64.3) issued WPAD lookups via both LLMNR and NBT-NS. Within milliseconds, 192.168.64.50 responded with Responder-style poisoned answers for both protocols. Seconds later, the Kali host attempted SMB NTLMSSP authentication to 192.168.64.50, indicating the poisoned responses redirected traffic to the attacker. Concurrently, Suricata detected ARP replies on eth0 where MAC 02:de:ad:be:ef:50 claimed ownership of the gateway IP 192.168.64.1, a classic ARP cache poisoning maneuver. At 16:16:59 the Wazuh agent on Kali stopped and restarted one second later, a possible attempt to blind the sensor. A second ARP spoof alert fired at 16:17:05. The attacker infrastructure (192.168.64.50) and the spoofed gateway MAC are the only external indicators; no accounts or hostnames were observed in the logs.
