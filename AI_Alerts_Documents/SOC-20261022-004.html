6. Attack Narrative

Over ~34 seconds, 22 distinct failure moments were recorded across sshd, PAM, and syslog channels, triggering two rule 100002 alerts (firedtimes 11 and 12). After a ~24-minute gap, a second burst from 192.168.64.3 (the agent's primary IP) produced 14 additional sshd failures and four rule 100002 alerts (firedtimes 1-4) within 14 seconds. Concurrently, two lightdm session-close events occurred at 17:12:51 UTC but are unrelated to the SSH activity. All attempts failed; no session was established.
