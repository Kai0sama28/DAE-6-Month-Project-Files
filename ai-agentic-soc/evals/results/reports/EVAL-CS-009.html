The first five events were reconnaissance and failed authentications against five distinct accounts (nadia.oconnor, dmitri.kim, olivia.weber, james.brown, tom.novak), mixing non-existent and valid usernames — consistent with password spraying (T1110.004) and password guessing (T1110.003). At 08:15:30 and 08:16:00, rules 5712 and 100031 aggregated the repeated failures from 198.51.100.30 against nadia.oconnor. At 08:17:00, a separate IP (198.51.100.33) successfully authenticated as ravi.khan, an account not previously targeted. The success triggered rule 40112. The disjoint IP and account suggest either credential reuse from a prior breach or a legitimate user logging in concurrently. No follow-on activity is visible in the correlated events.
