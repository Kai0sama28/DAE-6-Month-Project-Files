6. Attack Narrative

Between 08:13 and 08:16 UTC, three distinct source IPs attempted SSH logins against five accounts (james.brown x3, noah.mensah, tom.novak, nadia.oconnor, grace.rossi). Initial probes used non-existent usernames (rule 5710), followed by failed password attempts for valid users (rule 5760). Rule 5712 and the trigger rule 100031 aggregated the activity as a cross-account, single-source pattern. All authentication failed; no session establishment or privilege escalation occurred.
