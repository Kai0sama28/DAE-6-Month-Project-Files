An active Responder-style credential capture campaign targeted the Kali host (192.168.64.3) from attacker system 192.168.64.50 over an 11-minute window. 6. Attack Narrative

At 19:01 UTC the Kali host (192.168.64.3) logged in via lightdm. Minutes later, the attacker at 192.168.64.50 began broadcasting malicious LLMNR and NBT-NS responses (rules 100050, 100052) and spoofed the gateway MAC via ARP (rule 100059). The victim issued WPAD lookups over both LLMNR and NBT-NS (rules 100051, 100053), received the poisoned responses, and attempted NTLM authentication to the attacker's HTTP proxy (trigger rule 100057, HTTP 407) and SMB service (rule 100055). The attack spanned ~11 minutes. No logs indicate the NTLM hashes were successfully cracked or reused; auth_success_observed remains false.
