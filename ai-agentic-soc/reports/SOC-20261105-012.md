## 6. Attack Narrative

At 19:01:09 UTC the Kali host logged three lightdm GUI sessions. Two minutes later a burst of dpkg activity began (219 events in ~10 minutes), installing and removing packages consistent with tool staging. At 19:11:10 UTC Suricata captured an ARP reply from MAC 02:de:ad:be:ef:50 claiming to be the gateway 192.168.64.1 (rule 100059). Earlier in the window Suricata also saw Responder-style LLMNR and NBT-NS responses from 192.168.64.50 (rules 100050, 100052) and WPAD lookups from the Kali host itself over both protocols (rules 100051, 100053). Simultaneously, SCA checks recorded six SSH hardening controls flipping from pass to fail (rules 19011), reducing the host's CIS score below 50% (rule 19004). The combined evidence points to deliberate deployment of Responder or equivalent for credential harvesting via LLMNR/NBT-NS/ARP poisoning, with SSH weakening to facilitate persistent access.
